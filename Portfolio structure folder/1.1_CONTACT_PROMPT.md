# CONTACT SECTION — Contact.jsx

PURPOSE
Give recruiters and visitors an easy way to contact Subhankar.

CONTENT
- Email
- LinkedIn
- GitHub
- Other relevant social links
- Contact form if a real submission method is available

DESIGN
- Strong final CTA.
- Contact details on one side and form on the other on desktop.
- Stack on mobile.
- Keep the design consistent with the dark neon notebook theme.

ANIMATION
- Section heading reveals first.
- Contact cards/icons stagger in.
- Form fields reveal smoothly.
- Button has subtle hover feedback.
- Use focus states for keyboard users.

FUNCTIONALITY
- Email should be clickable.
- Social links should open correctly.
- Do not create a fake form submission system.
- If no backend/service is configured, make the form clearly configurable rather than pretending it sends messages.

MOBILE
- Large tap targets.
- No horizontal overflow.
