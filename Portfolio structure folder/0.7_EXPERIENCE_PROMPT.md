# EXPERIENCE SECTION — Experience.jsx

PURPOSE
Show internships, work experience and relevant practical experience.

EACH ENTRY
- Organization
- Role
- Duration
- Responsibilities
- Technologies/skills where applicable

DESIGN
- Professional timeline/card layout.
- Role and organization should be immediately visible.
- Responsibilities should be concise and scannable.

ANIMATION
- Timeline/entry reveal on scroll.
- Cards stagger vertically.
- Organization/role text appears before detailed bullets.
- Subtle hover border response.

MOBILE
- Single-column timeline.
- No horizontal overflow.

IMPORTANT
Do not invent employment dates, roles or responsibilities.
